OUR CELEBRATION OF GRACE

Our Little Girl, Growing Into a Beautiful New Chapter.

"மங்கல நங்கை என மலர்ந்து நிற்கும் எங்கள் குடும்பத்து நிலவு — டி. கே. மோனிஷா"

From taking her very first steps and filling our home with pure, contagious laughter, to blossoming into a graceful, intelligent, and warm-hearted young woman — every chapter of D. K. Monisha’s journey has been an eternal blessing to our family.

The Manjal Neerattu Vizha (பூப்புனித நீராட்டு விழா) is an ancient, cherished Tamil tradition where elders shower sacred turmeric water, love, and divine blessings, presenting her with her traditional silk half saree (Pavadai Dhavani) as she steps into womanhood.

01

Tradition & Grace

Adorned in pure silk, fragrant jasmine, and blessed with sacred mantras.

02

Elders' Blessings

Showered with boundless affection from parents, Periyappa, Periyamma, and relatives.

03

Joyous Celebration

Sacred rituals followed by live DJ celebration and traditional Virundhu.

Schedule of the Day

A step-by-step journey of traditional South Indian rituals, live DJ celebration, and grand feast.

09:00 AM

Mangala Isai & Welcome

மங்கல இசை மற்றும் வரவேற்பு

Welcoming our cherished family and guests with traditional Nadaswaram, Thavil, fresh rosewater, and Chandanam.

Traditional Manjal Neerattu Vizha

மஞ்சள் நீராட்டு மங்கல சடங்கு & முகூர்த்தம்

Sacred turmeric water ritual performed by maternal uncles (Thaai Maaman), aunts, Periyappa, Periyamma, and elders with Vedic chants and blessings, followed by Pavadai Dhavani (Half Saree) presentation.

Live DJ & Celebration
